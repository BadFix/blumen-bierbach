# Floral retail interaction study — 14 October 2026

The current public sites were inspected in a browser. The ideas below are adapted to an independent local florist; business promises, ratings and stock claims were not copied.

| Source | Observed | Applied to Bierbach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://www.bloomandwild.com/ | Favourites, image-led category shortcuts, seasonal collections | Browser-local favourites and a circular occasion rail |
| https://www.interflora.co.uk/ | Delivery-date/address/occasion selector; quick date choices | Quick requested dates in the inquiry basket, with explicit personal confirmation |
| https://www.fnp.com/ | Gift Finder entry, occasion/recipient/colour/budget navigation | Three-step flower finder for occasion, colour direction and freely chosen budget |
| https://www.1800flowers.com/ | Occasion navigation, gift categories and basket | A clearer selection summary, including a mobile basket bar; existing inquiry flow retained |
| https://flowwow.com/en/ | Browser required CAPTCHA; interactive detail inspection stopped. Public text previously showed photo-before-delivery feature | Optional photo request, explicitly subject to agreement; no photo service guarantee |

The greeting-card text preview, preset greetings, petal-inspired first-visit reveal, botanical line drawing in the finder, image fades and basket micro-interaction are original additions. No blocking loading screen is used. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation; the greeting preview represents text only, not a promised physical card design.

The flower finder uses a transparent local matching rule based on existing style categories; it is not an AI service or inventory check. No search data is sent to a server. Budgets remain requests, not confirmed sale prices. Address and location fields remain optional.
